# Oversteer

Oversteer reconstructs the observable Juiced 2 driving and career experience using
vehicles from the agreed source collections.

## Language

**Reference game**:
Juiced 2 on PS3 and Xbox 360, treated as a shared behavioral reference by project
direction.
_Avoid_: A blend of every Juiced 2 edition

**Vehicle model**:
A vehicle identity and its visual representation from the GT4, MC3, MCLA, or
Ford Racing 2 collections in the carmodels repository.
_Avoid_: Owned car (when referring to a model)

**Initial roster**:
The accepted set of 30 diverse vehicle models from the four agreed source
collections, shared by career and quick race.
_Avoid_: The complete donor catalog

**Career**:
The persistent single-player progression through events, rewards, vehicle
ownership, and customization toward game completion.

**Quick race**:
A standalone race selected directly rather than reached through career
progression.
